Privacy Policy

Chart Life · Last updated: November 3, 2026

Chart Life is a game made by Jelani Jonathans ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and what you can do about it. Short version: you play without an email address or password, we never ask for your real name, and all money in the game is fictional.

1. Who is responsible

Jelani Jonathans, the Netherlands. Contact: bagtobelt@protonmail.com.

2. What we collect

WhatWhy
A random player ID, created automatically the first time you play (an anonymous account, no email or password)To keep your game summary and leaderboard entry apart from other players'.
The player name you chooseShown to other players on the leaderboard and in the game.
Game data: in-game cash, net worth, level, app version, where your in-game money came from and went to (per source and per day, the last 60 money moves), and a history of your net worth (one point per hour)The leaderboard, support (for example fixing a bug that cost you in-game money), and finding cheating.
Trades on your live and prop accounts in the game: market, buy or sell, size, prices, times, result (demo trades are not collected)Checking that trades really happened at the prices on the chart, so the leaderboard stays fair.
Purchase records: the App Store transaction ID, the product, and when it was boughtChecking with Apple that a purchase is real, delivering it once, and handling refunds. We never see your payment details.
Technical data: your IP address and the time of each request, as seen by the services listed belowDelivering the service, security, and preventing abuse (for example limiting how many accounts one network can create).

We do not collect your real name, email address, contacts, photos, location, or microphone or camera data. Your game itself (your save) is stored on your own device.

3. What other players see

Players who were active in the last 30 days appear on the leaderboard with their player name, level, and net worth (exact for the top 10, rounded below that). Nothing else about you is shown to other players.

4. Services we use

  • Supabase (database hosting, servers in the European Union, Stockholm): stores the data in section 2.
  • Apple (App Store): processes purchases and confirms them to us. Apple's own privacy policy applies to your Apple ID and payments.
  • Google AdMob (advertising): shows ads in the game. In the EU and UK you are asked for consent first. On iOS you are asked whether apps may track you; if you say no, the advertising identifier is not shared. Ads may still be shown without personalization. See how Google uses information from apps.
  • Time services (apple.com, google.com, worldtimeapi.org, timeapi.io): the game reads the current time from them, because the game follows real time. They see your IP address, nothing else is sent.

We do not sell your information.

5. How long we keep it

  • Trades and net worth history: 30 days.
  • Your game summary and player name: as long as your account exists, or until you ask us to delete it.
  • Purchase records: as long as needed for refunds, disputes and the law (bookkeeping rules).

6. Legal basis (EU / UK)

We use your information to provide the game you asked for (contract), to keep the game fair and secure (legitimate interest), and, for personalized ads, with your consent. You can withdraw it at any time: in the game under Settings → Ad privacy, on iOS by turning off tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, or by contacting us.

7. Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it. Email bagtobelt@protonmail.com with your player ID (Settings → Player ID in the game), so we know which account is yours. In the EU you can also complain to your data protection authority (in the Netherlands: the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Deleting the app removes the save on your device. It does not delete the data on our servers: ask us for that.

8. Children

Chart Life is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 is playing, contact us and we will delete the data.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. For important changes we will tell you in the game.
